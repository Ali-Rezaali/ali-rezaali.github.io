## Introduction

Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) offers unprecedented monitoring capabilities. Unlike  optical satellites, radar penetrates cloud cover and operates day or night.

### Processing Pipeline

1. **Pre-processing**: Thermal noise removal and orbit file updates.
2. **Co-registration**: Aligning master and slave image pairs.
3. **Interferometry**: Extracting phase differences to measure millimeter-scale land movement.
