Core Disciplines

Research Focus & Methodology

🛰️

Multi-Sensor Remote Sensing

Time-series analysis using Sentinel-1/2, Landsat 8/9, MODIS, and VIIRS to monitor surface water dynamics, land cover change, and environmental indicators across arid and wetland ecosystems.

🌡️

Urban Heat & Environmental Risk

MSc thesis on spatiotemporal modeling of urban heat risk, integrating land surface temperature retrieval with socio-environmental variables to identify vulnerable urban areas.

🧠

Explainable GeoAI

Machine learning for spatial prediction — XGBoost and Scikit-Learn models paired with SHAP for interpretability, ensuring model outputs are transparent and defensible, not black-box.

🌍

Spatial Ecological Modeling

Species distribution and habitat suitability modeling, combining remote sensing-derived predictors with statistical frameworks to assess environmental change impacts on ecosystems.
