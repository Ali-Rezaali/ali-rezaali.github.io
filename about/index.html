Research Interests

Research Focus & Methodology

Earth Observation & Remote Sensing

Multi-sensor time-series analysis across Sentinel-1/2, Landsat 8/9, MODIS, and VIIRS — monitoring surface water dynamics, land cover change, and environmental indicators across arid and wetland ecosystems.

Explainable GeoAI

Machine learning for spatial prediction — XGBoost and Scikit-Learn pipelines paired with SHAP for interpretability, ensuring model outputs are transparent, defensible, and not black-box.

Environmental & Urban Hazard-Risk

IPCC-based hazard-risk frameworks for surface urban heat island and urban heat risk — integrating exposure and vulnerability into spatiotemporal models to identify environmentally vulnerable areas.

Spatiotemporal Modeling & Monitoring

Time-series analysis and dynamic change detection across environmental and urban systems, combining remote sensing-derived predictors with statistical and machine learning frameworks.
