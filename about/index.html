Research Focus & Methodology

📡

SAR & Microwave Remote Sensing

Sentinel-1 C-band & L-band SAR time-series analysis, coherence modeling, surface roughness estimation, and polarimetric decomposition for environmental monitoring.

🌍

Physical & Environmental Modeling

Radiative transfer modeling (PROSAIL/DART), Land Surface Temperature (LST) inversion, surface energy balance, and eco-hydrological dynamics across changing biomes.

⚡

Geospatial Deep Learning & Physics-Informed ML

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), PyTorch convolutional/transformer architectures for cloud masking, segmentation, and spatio-temporal downscaling.

💻

Cloud-Native Data Cubes & HPC

Processing terabyte-to-petabyte Earth Observation archives using Google Earth Engine, Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (COG), Zarr, and Slurm HPC clusters.
