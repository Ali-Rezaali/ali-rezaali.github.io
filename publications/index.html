Spatiotemporal Monitoring of Saline Water Body Changes Using Remote Sensing Data with a Focus on Comparing Spectral Indices (Case Study: Lake Urmia)

Ali Rezaali, Hamid Ebadi, Hadi Farhadi
Journal of Geomatics Science and Technology (2025) • DOI: 10.61186/jgst.14.3.69

A comparative multi-temporal evaluation of water indices (NDWI, NDVI, AWEISh, AWEInSh) on Landsat-8 imagery in Google Earth Engine (2018–2024) utilizing automated Edge Otsu thresholding. Proves NDWI achieved highest classification accuracy (99% OA, 0.97 Kappa) for separating saline surface water from salt crusts.
